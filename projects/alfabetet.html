A to Ö

When I had my son, I wanted something personal to hang on the wall in his room. Not just any print, but something made for him. That was how this project started.

I began with a simple idea: every letter would get an animal and a plant from Sweden. That worked well for a long time, until I reached the more unusual letters. Nature in Sweden doesn’t offer much for some of them, so I let the series grow beyond our own forests and meadows.

Every time I see my child’s print in his room, it makes me happy. I hope you get to feel the same joy when you hang one up at home.
