Illustrations

Helping Sambla find a warmer, more playful expression: 13 illustrations built on the new brand guidelines for their rebrand.

Deliverables
Illustrations
Client
Sambla Group
Brands
Sambla, Rahalaitos

The creative process

Based on the established creative brief, I created simplified, hand-drawn illustrations featuring organic and expressive shapes and action lines to convey movement. I worked within Sambla’s new colour palette and adapted the illustrations to create a cohesive visual language across the platform and app, ensuring they worked effectively across different contexts and backgrounds.

I had creative freedom throughout and refined the concepts in feedback sessions with the team, which left room to play and explore variations, like these doors opening in different ways.

The illustration set

Colour variations

Multiple colour variations were developed to ensure the illustrations could be adapted consistently across the different brands within Sambla Group, including Sambla and Rahalaitos.
